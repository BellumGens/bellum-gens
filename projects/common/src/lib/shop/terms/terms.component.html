The seller

Orders and prices

Prices are in euro and include all taxes. The total shown at checkout, including any discount and the shipping cost, is the amount you pay.

An order paid online is confirmed once the payment is received. An order paid on delivery is confirmed once it is placed. Either way, we send a confirmation to the email address you gave at checkout.

Payment

You can pay online or in cash on delivery.

Online payments are processed by Revolut. Cards, Revolut Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted. We never see your card details. Orders that are not paid in time are cancelled automatically and nothing is charged.

With cash on delivery (наложен платеж), you pay the order total to the courier when the parcel arrives. Paying on delivery costs nothing extra.

Delivery

We currently deliver by courier within Bulgaria only; we do not ship abroad. The shipping cost is shown before you place your order. We email you when the order is handed to the courier.

Right of withdrawal

You can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. To do so, email info@bellumgens.com with your order number.

Send the goods back within 14 days of telling us. Unless the goods are faulty, you pay the cost of returning them.

We refund everything you paid, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving your notice. Online payments are refunded to your original payment method; orders paid on delivery are refunded by bank transfer to an account you give us. We may wait until the goods are back with us before refunding.

Faulty goods

If something you received is faulty or not as described, contact us and we will repair, replace or refund it, in line with your statutory rights under the Bulgarian Consumer Protection Act.
