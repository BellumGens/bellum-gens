The Venue

The Summer Theater in Stara Zagora is located at the foot of the largest city park in Bulgaria. This open-air masterpiece combines nature and ancient traditions with modern architecture into a unique experience.

Players clash like gladiators on the open-air stage of this gorgeous theater, and you get the chance to meet your favorite pros face-to-face along the alleys of the beautiful park.

Stara Zagora

The city hosting this event has more than 8,000 years of history. Ancient forums, Roman murals and mosaics, 2,000-year-old theaters, bath houses, and aqueducts — it's all beneath your feet and all around you!
