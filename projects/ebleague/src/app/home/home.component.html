The Ultimate Gaming experience for your company

Covid-19 changed the way teams in your company bond. Esports Business League is a team building charity esports event, where your company team can compete against teams from other companies. Participants in the Esports Business League so far include companies like VMware, DXC, SAP, Experian, TELUS, Modis, HP, Infragistics, Chaos Group and many more!

What is Esports Business League?

Esports Business League is a charity esports event, which provides working gamers with an arena for competition between individuals and teams from different companies. Each season of the league is 4-8 weeks in length. Matches take place on week days after working hours Monday-Thursday. The league culminates with finals where the teams and players who have accumulated the most points during the season meet. All league matches are being broadcasted live on our Twitch channels.

Terms and conditions

group Team eligibility

All team members must be current employees of the registered company, with no active professional esports affiliation and no professional matches in the past 24 months. Standins must meet the same conditions.

person Individual players (SC2)

StarCraft II competitors must be current employees of their registered company. Substitutes may be added post-registration under the same eligibility conditions.

payments Registration fee

300 BGN per Counter-Strike team · 60 BGN per StarCraft II player. Paid via bank transfer after form submission. Limited to 16 teams / 32 players.

volunteer_activism Prize pool & charity

All registration fees form the prize pool, which is fully donated to a charitable cause chosen by the winners at the end of each season.

Tournament format

Counter-Strike League

  • 3-7 weeks
  • 2 groups with up to 8 teams each
  • 1-2 matches per team per week
  • Matches are in BO1 or BO3 format
  • Round robin format
  • Each team earns points weekly
  • Finals weekend with the 4 best teams from the group stage
  • Full information about the format can be found here

StarCraft II League

  • 3-7 weeks
  • 4 groups with up to 8 players each
  • 1-2 matches per player per week
  • Matches are in BO3 format
  • Round robin format
  • Each player earns points weekly
  • Finals weekend with the 8 best players from the group stage
  • Full information about the format can be found here

Prizes

🥇 1st Place — 75%

The winning team/player will appropriate 75% of the prize pool towards their choice of a charitable cause.

🥈 2nd Place — 25%

The runner-up will appropriate 25% of the prize pool towards their choice of a charitable cause.

About us

Esports Business League is organized by Bellum Gens — an organization focused on the development and popularization of esports in Bulgaria and on the Balkans. We want to make esports a recognizable topic for the business community and help all esports projects in the region grow.
